Privacy Policy

What the WOS EU1 bot and website store about you, why, and how to have it removed. Last updated 20 November 2026.

Who is responsible

The WOS EU1 staff team runs the WOS EU1 Discord bot, this website and the staff dashboard. If you have questions about your data, contact the staff in our Discord server.

What we store

  • Your Discord account: your user ID, username and display name, and, where needed, your roles in our server. We never get your email address or your password.
  • Bug reports and suggestions: what you write, the screenshots and videos you attach, the game area and version, and your chief name and player ID if you give them.
  • Linked game account: your in-game player ID, if you link it for gift codes, plus which codes were redeemed for you.
  • Moderation: in our Discord server, warnings, timeouts, kicks and bans, with the reason. We also keep message reports, appeals, and private notes our staff write about a member.
  • Recent chat messages in our Discord server: kept for a few days, 7 by default and never more than 30. This lets our staff see deleted or edited messages when moderating. They are deleted automatically after that.
  • Server activity: who invited whom to our server, giveaway entries, the language you chose, and votes such as “I have this too” on a known issue.
  • Website: if you log in with Discord, a login cookie that lasts up to 7 days. Your language choice is kept in your own browser. If you turn on notifications, we keep the technical address your browser gives us for them. To stop spam, we may keep a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address for a short time.
  • Promoter servers: the server’s ID, name and icon, the channels chosen, who added the bot, and which of our posts were sent there. We do not read or store messages in those servers.

Why we use it

We use this information to:

  • handle bug reports and pass real bugs on to the game’s developers;
  • find duplicate reports;
  • moderate and protect our community;
  • post announcements and gift codes;
  • redeem codes for you if you ask us to;
  • run the website.

We rely on our legitimate interest in running a safe, well-organised community. Where you choose a feature, such as linking your game account or notifications, we rely on your choice. You can switch it off again at any time.

Who else sees it

  • Our staff: moderators and admins of WOS EU1.
  • The game’s developers: they receive accepted bug reports with your chief name, player ID and attachments, but not your Discord name.
  • Google Cloud: hosts the bot and the website, stores backups on Google Drive, and translates report texts to English so duplicate reports can be found.
  • Discord: processes everything that happens in Discord itself.
  • The game’s gift code service: receives your player ID when the bot redeems a code for you.

We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Recent chat messages: deleted automatically after a few days, at most 30.
  • Login sessions: end after 7 days.
  • Reports, moderation history and the other records above: kept while the WOS EU1 server is running, unless you ask us to delete them.
  • Backups: replaced on a rolling basis.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • see the data we have about you;
  • correct it;
  • delete it, or limit how we use it.

You can also object to how we use it. Contact the staff in our Discord server. We may keep the minimum needed to keep a ban in place or to protect the community. If you are in the EU or EEA, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
